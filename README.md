##  Documented Variant Analysis
- **Mutation:** c.2209C>T  
- **Protein Change:** p.Arg737Ter (R737X)  
- **Mutation Type:** Nonsense Mutation  
- **Clinical Significance:** Severe Hemophilia A  
- **Mechanism:** Introduces a premature stop codon, truncating the protein at amino acid 737 and removing essential A3, C1, and C2 domains.  

---

##  Artificial Mutation Analysis
- **Mutation:** 1-nucleotide deletion at position 299  
- **Mutation Type:** Frameshift Deletion  
- **Effect:** Shifts the reading frame, scrambling all downstream amino acids and leading to a premature stop codon.  
- **Outcome:** Complete loss of functional domains and protein structure.
